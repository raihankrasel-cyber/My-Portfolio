PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you visit Raihan Kabir's digital marketing portfolio, contact Raihan, or use the portfolio's account and blog features.

Information you provide

If you send a project note, the site sends the name, email address, and message you enter to its contact processing service so Raihan can respond. If you create or use a site account, account details such as your email address and authentication session are processed by Supabase, the site's authentication provider. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in a project note or public post.

Website operation and analytics

The site uses browser storage to remember display and language preferences. Google Tag Manager is installed and may load analytics or advertising tags configured for the site; Vercel Web Analytics may also collect aggregate site usage information. These services may process technical information such as browser, device, page, and visit data. Their operation is subject to their own privacy terms and the tags enabled for this site.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested services.
  • To operate account, sign-in, and blog features.
  • To maintain, understand, and improve the portfolio.
  • To protect the site from spam, misuse, and security issues.

Sharing and retention

Information is processed by service providers that support site hosting, contact delivery, authentication, and analytics. It is not sold. These providers may process information in locations outside your country. Information is retained for as long as needed for the purposes above, subject to provider settings and applicable requirements.

Your choices

You can contact Raihan to ask about a message or account associated with you, or to request correction or deletion where applicable. You can also manage cookies and browser storage through your browser settings; some site preferences may then be forgotten.

External websites and contact

The portfolio links to third-party websites such as Medium, Telegram, and WhatsApp. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. For privacy questions, contact raihan.k.rasel@gmail.com.
